My open source Guard tool won 8th place in the international Kiro Hackathon hosted by Dynamous.ai and AWS Kiro!

Guard is a CLI/TUI tool that protects your files from unauthorized modifications by AI coding agents. It gives you control and peace of mind that the AI doesn't mess with working code.

The hackathon was a great experience building with the help of Kiro and a large sponsored token budget to experiment with. What impressed me most was the quality of the plans that Kiro generates.

During the livestream a judge said "I genuinely consider using this myself."

Many thanks to the Dynamous.ai team and AWS Kiro for putting this together!
